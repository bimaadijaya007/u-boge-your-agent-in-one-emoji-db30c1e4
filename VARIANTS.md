# Alternatives considered

Two other glyphs were seriously considered before settling on 🐺.
Each is good; each loses to the wolf for one specific reason.

## 🦉 The owl

The owl is wise, nocturnal, and watches while others sleep — a fair
portrait of an agent that monitors bounties overnight.

Why it loses: the owl *waits and watches*, but it does not *hunt* —
it sits, observes, and reacts, while this agent actively chases
bounties down, so the owl describes only the quiet half of the job.

## 🕷️ The spider

The spider works in the dark, weaves patiently, and catches things
while nobody is looking — a fair portrait of background automation
that sets traps (watches, cron jobs, retries) and harvests results.

Why it loses: the spider's story is menace and ambush, and it waits
for prey to come to it, while this agent goes out and takes the
bounty — so the spider gets the darkness right but the direction
of the hunt wrong.
